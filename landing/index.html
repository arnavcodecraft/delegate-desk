FAQ

Questions, answered

Something else? Talk to us.

Is DelegateDesk really free?

Yes. Organisers list and run conferences for free, and delegates use Prep and the Masterclass for free. Paid extras for big organisers are coming later; everything free today stays free.

Do delegate fees come to us?

Yes. Payments go straight into your own account by UPI, bank transfer or your payment gateway. DelegateDesk records them and handles proofs, receipts and refunds.

Do delegates need an account to register?

No. They can register from your link. With a free account they also see every conference they joined, their allotments, a MUN CV and Prep.

Can the whole EB chair together?

Yes. Every member of the dais opens the committee room on their own device with their own link, and marks show who gave them.

What if the venue Wi-Fi drops?

The apps open without internet, and you can download an offline copy of your conference with every delegate, seat and payment.

I organise and I am also a delegate. Do I need two accounts?

No. One sign-in opens both your organiser dashboard and your delegate profile.
